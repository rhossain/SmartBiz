- (decision) Purchase Cost modelled as a property of received units (a Stock-In), not a field on Product. This is what makes 'profit truthful by construction' structural rather than aspirational, and it is why Inventory Staff cannot be cost-blind
- (decision) No numeric parser-accuracy target in the PRD. A percentage would be untestable without a labelled corpus of real Bangladeshi seller messages, which the project does not have and will not fabricate. Replaced with a testable BEHAVIOURAL contract: extraction always visible, always editable, never auto-submitted, never blocking. Accuracy measurement deferred to Open Questions
- (decision) Returned status and return-to-stock SEPARATED into two events (FR-13 and FR-14). Returned is a commercial statement; stock restoration is a physical-receipt event recording condition (resalable vs damaged) and supporting partial receipt. Satisfies scope.md rule 'returned products restore inventory only when physically received' and fixes the most common cause of wrong inventory
- (decision) Stock allocated at CONFIRMED, not at order creation, so an abandoned New order never holds inventory. Insufficient stock does not block saving a New order but requires explicit acknowledgement at confirmation
- (decision) Settlement is ONE-TO-ONE by design: one Settlement Row settles at most one Order and vice versa. Every real-world deviation (short payment, grouped row, split across batches) becomes a Settlement Variance with a mandatory Disposition (Accepted/Disputed/Written Off). Rejected a full many-to-many allocation model as exceeding the Hero cap. Trade recorded openly as NOTE FOR PM: auditable rather than elegant
- (decision) Audit trail and reversal unified into one construct (FR-63, FR-64): every change is an Audit Entry, reversal is always a Compensating Entry, history is append-only, and NO interface exists anywhere for editing or deleting an Audit Entry or Inventory Movement. Serves both the scope.md audit-retention rule and the seller's fear-of-mistakes barrier with a single mechanism
- (decision) Reversal refuses where a dependent record would be invalidated (e.g. reversing a Stock-In whose units are already consumed by a Delivered Order), naming the dependency, rather than permitting it and leaving inconsistency. A reversal is itself not reversible
- (decision) Stock changes ONLY via immutable typed Inventory Movements, and summing them must reproduce the current Stock Level exactly - asserted as an automated test invariant (NFR-7, FR-56). This is what makes both audit and reversal structurally sound rather than bolted on
- (decision) Success metrics deliberately written as demonstration- and test-verifiable rather than market/adoption metrics. Reason: academic submission with no real users, so adoption metrics would be unfalsifiable. 5 primary, 4 secondary, 4 counter-metrics
- (insight) Counter-metrics carry unusual weight in this product: SM-C1 forbids optimising parser coverage and SM-C2 forbids optimising automatic settlement match rate, because both would trade confident wrongness for apparent capability - the exact failure the Completeness Indicator exists to prevent. SM-C4 forbids reducing visible incompleteness to make the dashboard look cleaner
- (decision) NFR performance targets sized to the real target user (50 orders/day, ~18k/year, 1 business, 5 concurrent members) rather than invented scale. Prevents architecture over-engineering against a free-tier constraint
