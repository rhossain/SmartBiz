SmartBiz BD — Solution Design

A Bangla-first order-to-cash system for Bangladeshi SME sellers, and the architecture that makes its money figures trustworthy.

Step 5 of 11 · Architecture 8 September 2026 24 architecture decisions 82 FR · 28 NFR · 15 metrics Solo build, free-tier deployment

This document explains the architecture to a reader who has not seen the code. The authoritative artifact is ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md beside this file — call it the architecture spine; it is terse by design and states rules without arguing for them. This document carries the argument — what each decision prevents, and what would have gone wrong without it.

1 · The problem the architecture has to solve

A small Bangladeshi seller ships cash-on-delivery (COD) through third-party couriers. They hand a parcel to a courier and are paid days or weeks later, minus fees, sometimes minus a shortfall nobody explains. The money exists in three states at once: earned (delivered, not yet paid out), realized (actually received), and lost (never reconciled). Their entire question is which of this money is actually mine?

That question makes this a harder engineering problem than building a CRUD order tracker, and it does so in one specific way: almost every number on the screen is derived, and several of them can be derived more than one way. Profit depends on which cost flowed, which basis revenue is recognized on, and how much of the data is even present. A system where two screens answer the same question differently is worse than no system, because the seller trusts it before discovering it lied.

So the architecture's job is narrow and specific: make divergence structurally impossible. Not discouraged by convention, not caught in review — impossible, or at least loud. Almost every decision below is that job in a different costume.

3 · The paradigm, and the three things it buys

A layered modular monolith with a derived-state ledger core and ports-and-adapters at every optional transport. One Django project, one process, one database. Every capped capability is a Django app owning its own tables; the seams between them are enforced by dependency rules rather than by network boundaries. Concretely: Django 6.1 with Gunicorn, PostgreSQL 17, React 19 over Inertia, and WeasyPrint for documents — full versions and the deployment topology are in §11.

Why a monolith is the right answer here, not the lazy one

Microservices would cost the thing this product cannot lose. Confirming an order writes an inventory movement, updates a stock projection, snapshots a cost and writes an audit entry — and either all four happen or none do (NFR-6). Across a service boundary that becomes a distributed transaction, or a saga, or a silent inconsistency. At the stated volume of around 50 orders a day, a service boundary buys nothing and costs atomicity. On a 512 MB instance it is not even available.

Why the ledger core is the load-bearing choice

Stock and weighted-average cost are not stored values that code updates. They are folds over an immutable, insert-only table of typed movements — event sourcing applied to one aggregate, in two dimensions at once. This single decision hands the design four things for free: reversal without deletion (FR-64), a complete audit trail (FR-63), reproducibility of any historical figure, and a reconciliation test that can prove the projections are correct rather than merely plausible.

Why ports at the optional seams

The PRD marks the AI assistant, courier integration, OCR and email as optional — the product must be whole without any of them. Treated as ordinary dependencies, "optional" decays into "required" the moment a concrete client is imported into a core flow. Treated as ports, the fallback adapter is always present, always built, and covered by a full test pass with every provider absent.

4 · Module structure

Sixteen Django apps in five strictly-ordered tiers. A module may call only modules strictly below itself. The rule that makes this workable is a distinction most layering rules omit:

A schema reference is legal in any direction. A ForeignKey declared as a lazy string — "orders.Order" — is how the entity diagram is built. Forbidding these would mean rebuilding referential integrity by hand.

A behavioral dependency is forbidden upward. Importing another module's models, managers or querysets to read or write through them is out. Cross-module behavior goes through that module's services and selectors, and nothing else.

The decision inside this diagram that took the longest to get right

The first draft put derived balances where they intuitively belong: Customer Due in customers, Supplier Balance in suppliers. Both immediately needed to read upward — a customer's due depends on their orders, which sit a tier above. Every fix that preserved the intuition also punched a hole in the dependency graph.

The resolution: money owns every figure derived from more than one module, and identity modules hold records only — never a balance about themselves. Customer Due, Supplier Balance, Money With Couriers, Unsettled Profit, Collection Status, Customer Risk Ratio and Aging Buckets all live in one module.

That one relocation closed four separate findings at once: the missing upward edge, the tier violation, the seven derived balances that had no declared owner and could each have been computed in two places, and an original business rule about customer dues that had no home anywhere in the design.

Sixteen apps, fifteen capped capabilities

The counts deliberately do not match, and saying so is cheaper than an artificial merge: tenancy and accounts together serve one capability row, and money serves the dashboard row plus every derivation named above. The capability map in the spine is authoritative over the directory listing.

5 · The data model

Twenty-four entities. Two properties of the shape matter more than the entity list:

  1. No entity stores a derived balance. There is no DUE table, no supplier-balance column, no profit column. Every one of those is computed. A stored balance is a second source of truth, and a second source of truth is the defect this whole system exists to avoid.
  2. The movement table carries two signed dimensions — quantity and cost — which is what makes §6 work.

Integrity is declared in the database, not only in services

The most efficient way to void the entire ledger paradigm is one line: on_delete=CASCADE. A Django cascading delete never calls Model.delete(), so no amount of service-layer discipline can stop it. The insert-only guarantee dies silently, and every reconciliation test still passes because the rows are simply gone.

So integrity that must hold regardless of code path is declared where no code path can bypass it: PROTECT on anything a ledger or audit row points at; named unique constraints on SettlementMatch(order), CompensatingEntry(original), customer phone per business, variant combination, and settlement-import content hash; check constraints for movement sign and non-negative projections. A data migration touching money or stock writes audit entries exactly as a service would.

6 · Stock, cost and locking — the ledger, and the bug that nearly shipped

The first draft of this architecture had an asymmetry that looked harmless and was not. Quantity was an immutable fold over movements. Cost was a stored, mutable weighted average. Both descriptions sounded correct.

The failure, concretely. Buy 10 units at ৳100, then 10 more at ৳120. Sell 8 at the blended ৳110. Now reverse the first purchase — a normal correction, one of the seven reversible operations.

  • The quantity check passes: 12 remaining ≥ 10 being removed. Nothing objects.
  • The stored average, asked to un-mix a blend it no longer has the inputs for, becomes ৳160.
  • The truth is ৳120. Every subsequent order line is now costed wrong, and every profit figure with it.

The requirement (FR-64) claims the cost contribution "backs out cleanly." That claim is arithmetically false unless cost is a fold too. No test in the suite would have caught it, because every individual assertion still held.

Two smaller consequences follow, and both were gaps until this was fixed. Derived unit costs need four decimal places, not two — ৳1,073.85 over 10 units is ৳107.3850, and rounding that to two leaves five poisha permanently unreconciled. And purchase cost is nullable with no default: a 0.00 default would silently void the completeness indicator in the one direction that flatters the seller falsely.

Concurrency across shared balances

Two orders confirmed simultaneously must not both allocate the last unit. Every read-then-write over a shared balance takes select_for_update(), with the comparison inside the lock and inside the same transaction as the write. Four lock classes are named — stock projections, Customer Due, Supplier Balance, and settlement batch close — under one global ordering (declared table order, then primary key) so that no two code paths can acquire them in opposite sequence and deadlock.

7 · Settlement — where a passing test suite hides a double count

A courier remits a lump sum against many parcels. The module that reconciles it is the one the product exists for, and it has two failure modes that are invisible to ordinary testing.

One: a matcher that spreads one ৳4,500 remittance across several orders to make the arithmetic close. Every total balances. The discrepancy the module exists to surface has been absorbed instead.

Two, subtler: a grouped row raises an accepted ৳3,000 variance, which gets added to realized profit — and then added again when the sibling orders settle. Both additions are individually correct. The figure is wrong.

So: a match links exactly one settlement row to exactly one order, and is unique on the order at the database level. Cash is recognized once, at the match. A variance is a reconciliation record and never a second cash input — its disposition adjusts the receivable, not revenue. Partial and grouped remittances resolve into one-to-one matches plus variances, never a fractional allocation.

Import is idempotent on a content hash unique per business, so re-uploading the same payout file is recognized rather than re-applied. And a batch cannot close while holding an undispositioned variance — checked inside the batch lock, because otherwise a close racing a match strands the batch in exactly the state this rule forbids, with no route out through the interface.

Derived figures carry their own provenance

Profit is not one number. It has a recognition basis (earned or realized), a period key, and a completeness qualifier. Every derivation returns all four — value, basis, period key and completeness — as one inseparable result — a caller cannot obtain the bare value. Fixing only the return shape is not enough: two conforming callers still diverge if one buckets by order creation and the other by delivery. So the period key is fixed per basis (earned buckets by delivery, realized by settlement date) and the basis per surface is fixed by the PRD, not chosen at the call site.

8 · The money gate

Cost and margin are exactly what a seller does not want a helper to see. PRD §6.2 is a 31-row capability matrix across five roles — and it is emphatically not a ladder: the manager sees every figure but no audit trail, and inventory staff see purchase cost while being denied margin. Neither fits above or below the other.

One capability function, a per-cell lookup against the matrix, is the single gate — call it the capability gate. Two things about its reach had to be found rather than assumed:

Reach one — every egress surface, not just pages. This stack renders React components from JSON props. Hiding a cost column in a component leaves the cost sitting in the props, one devtools panel away. So the gate filters the payload: Inertia props, CSV exports, all three PDFs, the daily summary image and text, and assistant answers. A test asserts, per role and per surface, that no denied cell appears in rendered output.

Reach two — derivable quantities, not just named fields. The requirements produce a live instance of this: §6.2 grants inventory staff purchase cost and withholds margin. Margin is the relationship between cost and selling price — so any surface showing that role a selling price beside a cost hands them the withheld figure by subtraction, while each field individually is compliant. The denied figure therefore constrains what the permitted ones may sit beside.

That second point resolved a conflict rather than merely adding a rule: it is what preserves the pack slip as a genuine, deliberate exception — the one place inventory staff legitimately see an amount to collect, because they are otherwise denied the COD figure and cannot hand over the parcel without it.

What the client may remember

A shop phone is shared. If role-filtered props or computed figures persisted client-side, a helper opening the app after the owner would see the owner's numbers — inverting the gate entirely, with no server involved. So the client persists exactly one thing: the seller's own unsent input — a client-held draft — per device, keyed to the member and cleared on logout, role change and member switch. No figure, aggregate or report is ever cached. A cold open shows a loading state and fetches; it does not show a remembered number.

9 · Bangla rendering — one pipeline, and why

Bangla is a complex script: it needs conjunct formation and vowel-sign repositioning. A browser does this natively. Most PDF and image libraries do not.

Two renderers mean two font stacks and two shaping paths — and conjuncts come out correct in the invoice and broken in the summary card. The breakage is discovered late, because the person testing usually cannot read the script closely enough to notice a malformed conjunct in a thumbnail.

So one HTML and CSS template set is rendered by WeasyPrint to PDF, and the daily summary image is that same PDF rasterized by pypdfium2. One font file, one shaping stack — Pango with HarfBuzz. No second renderer, no client-side canvas text, no Chromium in the production image. Playwright is a test-only dependency.

This requirement is also the reason the deployment looks the way it does: Pango is a system library, so the application needs a container host. That single dependency chain — FR-68 → Pango → Docker → a container platform — determined the entire operational envelope in §11.

Formatting has one owner, with one exemption

Digit, currency and date formatting live in localization, and every surface calls it — the React formatter is a thin binding over the same contract, never a parallel implementation. The exemption is human identifiers: ORD-1042 stays in Latin digits, because a seller reads it aloud to a courier. And text the seller types — product names, categories, notes — is bilingual data stored in paired columns, not translations in a message catalog.

10 · The assistant, and the two things it may not do

The assistant answers ten fixed questions in Bangla. The architecture inverts the usual arrangement.

Not this: a model that queries the database and writes prose about what it found. Such a model can leak another business's rows, and can route around the capability gate — because it is the thing doing the reading.

This: the application computes every figure and hands the model pre-formatted strings to phrase. A model that cannot see the data cannot misreport it, and there is no gate for it to route around.

Two hard rules

  • The model never originates a number. Returned text is validated before display: every numeral in it must appear in the supplied figure set, or templated phrasing is used instead. The failure it prevents comes free with a language model — a confident figure nobody computed.
  • The outbound payload is allow-listed by construction. The builder's parameters are exactly question text, query name, and already-computed aggregates. It accepts no object, no queryset, no field dictionary and no keyword passthrough — a payload containing a customer field cannot be built, and a test asserts that. Free-tier model terms generally permit the provider to retain and learn from submissions, which is why this is enforced structurally rather than by a review checklist.

11 · Operational envelope

Every figure and platform claim here was verified against a live vendor source on 8 September 2026, not recalled. Two changed the design.

The two verified facts that changed decisions

512 MB and 0.1 CPU. An earlier draft of this architecture recorded this figure as unpublished, having failed to find it. It is published — Render renamed "instance types" to "compute plans," and the old URL 404s. The correction matters because 512 MB is now a hard design constraint rather than an unknown: one Gunicorn worker, and every operation whose memory use grows with its input — PDF rendering, spreadsheet import — must declare its bound.

Render's own free PostgreSQL expires 30 days after creation. This is why the database is deliberately not Render's. Neon's free tier persists indefinitely, which for an artifact that must remain demonstrable is the difference between working and a dead link.

What the free tier costs the design

Credential recovery

Registration issues a single-use recovery code, shown exactly once and stored only as a salted hash. Reset requires the code plus a new password. The consequence is stated plainly in delivery documentation rather than hidden: a lost code makes the account unrecoverable without direct database access. On a platform that blocks outbound mail, the alternative is not a better reset flow — it is no reset flow.

12 · Testing strategy

Every architectural rule phrased "a test asserts" has a named test under tests/invariants/. Five of those suites deserve mention because they test things ordinary testing cannot reach.

SuiteWhat it proves, and why nothing else can
Ledger reconciliationReplays the movement table and asserts both projections — quantity and cost pool — match the fold. A single-dimension version of this test passes despite the stored-average error in §6.
Role × surface matrixIterates all five roles against every egress surface and asserts no denied cell appears in the output bytes. Manual checking cannot cover 5 roles × 31 capabilities × 6 surface types, and the leak is an omission rather than a mistake — exactly the failure mode manual review is worst at.
No-adapter passThe full suite runs green with every optional provider absent. This is the only test that proves an absent provider degrades rather than breaks.
Payload constructionAsserts an outbound model payload containing a customer field cannot be constructed. A negative test about an API shape, not about behavior.
Contrast and control boundariesPorted from the prototype. Asserts WCAG floors arithmetically off the design tokens, and that no interactive control draws its boundary in a decorative rule color.

The contrast suite is what chose the styling stack. Contrast is fully determined by the tokens and invisible to every other harness — a caption at 2.9:1 reads as quiet, not broken, so screenshots and human review both forgive it. The prototype's harness is the only enforcement that exists.

Under a CSS-in-JS or utility-class stack, 2,254 lines of already-validated CSS would be rewritten and every floor re-derived by hand. So styling is plain CSS with custom properties, the tokens live in one file tested against the design spine for drift, and class names stay string-literal so the static scan still resolves them. That is a testing requirement choosing a stack, which is the right way round.

13 · Delivery, and the floor that does not move

Independent review put this build at 8–12 weeks for one developer, against a 2–4 week window. The interesting part is not the number — it is where the overrun sits.

The PRD's retreat path cuts feature depth: drop capture paths, drop report ranges. But the likely overruns are in things that cannot retreat — the ledger, the settlement matcher, the capability gate, Bangla shaping. Retreating depth does not relieve pressure on any of them.

Without a declared floor, two epics under time pressure each decide independently what may be dropped, and the first casualty is always a test, because dropping one breaks nothing visible today. The result is a broad system that is quietly wrong — a worse artifact than a narrow system that is right.

So the floor is declared and does not retreat: one hero flow end to end — capture, confirm, ship, settle — carrying the two-dimensional ledger, the settlement matcher, the gate over every egress surface, one Bangla PDF through the single pipeline, and the invariant test suite. No scope reduction may be funded from it. Everything above it retreats in the PRD's declared order, and a dropped capability moves to the v2 list rather than being half-built.

14 · How this architecture was reviewed

The spine was put through a review gate before being called done: a deterministic structural lint, then seven independent lenses run in parallel — three of them one per load-bearing input document, plus a rubric walker, a version-verification lens, an adversarial lens, and a data-integrity lens. The adversarial lens was instructed to construct two implementations that obey every rule to the letter and still build incompatibly.

The gate returned revise, and the findings clustered into one repeated shape rather than scattering:

The spine fixed what each rule was and left its arity, its ownership and its period key unstated. Six of the twenty decisions the gate reviewed failed that way — four more were added afterward, closing exactly the gaps this pass found, which is how the total reached twenty-four. Two compliant implementations could each satisfy every word written and still disagree about how many, whose, or over what window.

Most fixes were therefore one clause added to an existing decision, not a new decision — which is a useful signal about where architectural rules tend to be underspecified.

Findings that changed the design rather than clarifying it:

  • The cost ledger (§6) — found independently by two lenses.
  • The absence of any database-level integrity policy (§5) — also found twice.
  • Seven derived balances with no declared owner (§4).
  • The gate reaching only page props (§8).
  • The presentation dimension being largely silent — no styling technology, no home or test for inherited design tokens, no owner for client drafts (§12).
  • A published platform figure this architecture had recorded as unpublished (§11).

Not every finding was accepted. One lens flagged the pinned model identifier as unverified; the verification lens had confirmed that exact identifier against live vendor documentation the same day, so the objection was rejected on evidence rather than split down the middle. Recording that is part of the point: a review gate that accepts everything is not a gate.

17 · What this architecture deliberately does not decide

A spine that decides everything is a spine that will be wrong about most of it. The inclusion test throughout was: could two modules built independently choose incompatibly here, is the call non-obvious, and is it a real trade-off? Where the answer to any of the three was no, the decision is named and left open.

  • Per-story internals — form objects, parser rule organization, report query shapes. Owned by the epics and then by the code.
  • The variant stock-key representation — composite axis string or normalized rows. Bounded rather than open: whichever table exists must take a declared position in the global lock order.
  • Caching — nothing is cached beyond unsent input. At this volume a query plan is cheaper than a stale figure. Revisit only if a measured report breaches its budget after the statement timeout is in place.
  • An OCR provider — the no-provider path is already a complete feature, so this cannot block delivery.
  • Quota reporting — the behaviour is fixed; only whether exhaustion is surfaced to the owner remains open.
  • Rate limiting, log aggregation, error monitoring, CI topology — none motivated by one business and five members on a capped free tier. The content of the test floor is not deferred, only where it runs.
  • Per-business provider keys and model selection — v2. The port is shaped so this is configuration, not redesign.

One assumption remains open, with a stated confirmation condition: Docker build support on the free instance specifically is established by omission from an exclusion list rather than by an affirmative statement. It is confirmed at first deploy.

That is the last open thread in an architecture built to close them. Everywhere else, the answer to which of this money is actually mine is now structural rather than a matter of trust. What remains is building it.
