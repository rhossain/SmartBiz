- (decision) Stance: Creative Partner. Batch: Question Storming, Assumption Reversal, One Feature Only, Trickster's Gambit
- (technique) started Question Storming
- (direction) User prefers not to generate freely; run Creative Partner via concrete multiple-choice questions, one focused round at a time
- (decision by user) Primary v1 user: Facebook/WhatsApp seller (1-3 people, 10-50 orders/day, COD-heavy, no storefront)
- (insight by user) User rejected picking one wedge pain: COD reconciliation, DM order chaos, unknown profit and stock accuracy are ALL the wedge
- (idea by coach) Reframe: the four pains are one causal chain (DM order -> wrong stock -> cancel/return -> COD mismatch -> unknown profit). Differentiator = closing the loop DM-to-cash, not shipping 12 modules
- (idea by coach) Positioning candidate: 'the order-to-cash ledger for BD online sellers' - every order traceable to money actually received
- (decision by user) Order entry: paste-the-DM auto-parse as primary, fast manual form as always-available fallback
- (idea by coach) Clipboard as the integration: parsing pasted Bangla/Banglish DM blobs replaces Messenger/WhatsApp APIs that are out of scope
- (decision by user) Device reality: mixed - phone capture during the day, laptop for reconciliation/reports at night. Both layouts must genuinely work
- (technique) started Assumption Reversal
- (decision by user) All four reversals approved for v1: courier CSV settlement import, Bangla-first default, cost captured at stock-in, offline-tolerant order entry
- (decision by coach) Cost-gap policy (delegated to coach): show profit with an explicit completeness flag (e.g. 'based on 82/94 items') plus one-tap jump to fill missing costs. Rejected blocking reports and default-margin guessing
- (question) TENSION for PRD: 12 MVP modules + 4 reversals vs scope.md section 9 'medium-complexity' constraint - what actually ships in v1?
- (technique) started One Feature Only
- (decision by user) SPINE: Order-to-Cash Ledger - every order traceable from DM to money actually received. All other modules are inputs to this thread
- (decision by user) No module is cut; depth is tiered. HERO: orders, COD+CSV settlement, dashboard money view. FULL: auth/roles, customers, products+variants, inventory, expenses, dues, suppliers, all 9 reports. THIN: AI insights only
- (direction by user) User wants all realistic features that attract buyers - avoid hollow modules in the demo; sellability is a design criterion
- (decision by coach) Customer risk score = simple computed ratio (return rate + due history), not ML (scope section 7 excludes advanced ML)
- (technique) started Trickster's Gambit
- (decision by user) All trickster moves adopted: zero-setup (order creates product inline), audit-log-as-undo, forwardable Bangla daily summary, and DM+courier-CSV as the real data-entry engine with seller only correcting
- (insight by coach) Forwardable Bangla summary doubles as an organic growth loop - every forward markets to another seller
- (technique) started convergence via MoSCoW
- (decision by coach) MoSCoW MUST: order-to-cash spine, fast manual entry, rule-based paste-DM parse, courier CSV settlement import w/ auto-match, Bangla-first default, responsive phone+laptop, cost-at-stock-in, profit completeness flag, zero-setup inline product create, audit-log-as-undo, all 12 modules at tiered depth
- (decision by coach) MoSCoW SHOULD: forwardable Bangla daily summary, computed customer risk score, thin AI canned Q&A. WON'T: all scope section 7 integrations, ML risk models, billing implementation
- (decision by user) Offline scope reduced from full sync to resilient local drafts (autosave in-progress order, restore after drop) - no sync engine, protects section 9 medium-complexity constraint
- (decision by coach) Paste-DM parser must be rule-based (phone regex + address heuristics), no paid AI dependency, per section 9 free-tier constraint
- (insight by coach) SYNTHESIS 1: Order-to-Cash spine reconciles 'want everything' with prioritization - modules stop competing and become inputs to one thread
- (insight by coach) SYNTHESIS 2: paste-DM parse + courier CSV import + zero-setup product create are one principle: 'the seller should never type what the system could have read'. This is the real differentiator, not any single feature
- (insight by coach) SYNTHESIS 3: forwardable Bangla daily summary is a growth loop disguised as a reporting feature - organic seller-to-seller distribution
- (insight by coach) CHOSEN DIRECTION: SmartBiz BD v1 = Bangla-first order-to-cash operating system for BD Facebook/WhatsApp sellers; 12 modules at tiered depth; wins on data-entry avoidance and COD money truth
