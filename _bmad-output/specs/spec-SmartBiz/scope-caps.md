# Module Depth Caps

Fifteen capability rows — three Hero, twelve Full, nothing at Thin. The cap binds in both directions: exceeding the ceiling is out of scope even if it is a good idea and must be paid for by a funded reduction elsewhere (SPEC.md Constraints); falling short of the ceiling is incomplete. Story acceptance criteria may not exceed the *Does not include* column. Full detail and rationale: PRD §4.

## Hero — the demonstration centrepiece, deep behaviour, tested most thoroughly

| Capability | Ceiling — must reach | Does not include at this tier |
|---|---|---|
| **CAP-1 Order management** | Paste-to-parse capture with correction, fast manual entry, inline Product creation, five additional capture paths in the stated build order (FR-73–77), full Order Status lifecycle, cancellations, returns with condition routing, discounts, delivery charge, per-Order unit economics, supplying an absent Purchase Cost at any status (FR-69) | Order editing after Delivered beyond status/settlement/the FR-69 carve-out; partial shipment; order splitting or merging; recurring orders; quotations; persisted screenshots; multi-message paste (FR-78, v2); any capture path requiring a paid service; a parser that learns from corrections |
| **CAP-2 COD and delivery** | Courier records, Column Mapping per Courier, settlement file import, automatic matching, Settlement Variance detection with mandatory Disposition, unmatched-row resolution, simulated courier behind a real adapter interface | Live courier API calls; courier rate cards or charge prediction; pickup scheduling; multi-courier automatic assignment; label printing |
| **CAP-3 Dashboard** | Money With Couriers, realized vs pending cash, profit with Recognition Basis and Completeness Indicator, low-stock and dues attention lists, all Role-filtered | Configurable widgets/layouts; custom date-range comparison; saved views; charts beyond the money story; forecasting |

## Thin — retained as a named retreat destination, not a shipped tier

No capability ships at Thin in v1. AI-assisted insights (CAP-15) held the only Thin row before being promoted to Full by explicit user decision (PRD §4.3); its Thin shape — free-text box, button-list routing, templated phrasing, no model layer — is not hypothetical, because §4.2's Full ceiling already requires that exact surface to exist as CAP-15's no-provider fallback. Retreating CAP-15 to Thin under schedule pressure (PRD §4.4 step 3) therefore removes the model layer rather than building anything new.

This is the retreat destination named by [AD-24 in the architecture spine](../../planning-artifacts/architecture/architecture-SmartBiz-2026-09-08/ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md) for CAP-15 specifically. AD-24's floor itself does not retreat — see SPEC.md Constraints.
