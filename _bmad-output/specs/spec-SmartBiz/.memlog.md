- (constraint) Module depth caps bind in both directions (PRD section 4): exceeding a capability's ceiling is out of scope even if a good idea and must be paid for by a funded reduction elsewhere (section 8); falling short of the ceiling is incomplete. This is the constraint that makes 15 capabilities buildable in the stated window at all.
- (constraint) Five counter-metrics (PRD SM-C1..C5) forbid optimizing parser extraction breadth, automatic settlement match rate, module count/breadth, visible-incompleteness smoothing, and insight-routing coverage — each counterbalances a primary metric where the naive optimization produces a confidently wrong figure. These are genuine constraints: they rule out specific implementation choices a developer would otherwise be tempted toward.
- (constraint) Free-tier only, no paid service required for any Hero flow, including with email unconfigured (NFR-20, FR-43, section 8) — binds hosting, the credential-recovery mechanism (AD-19), and the AI/OCR provider seams (AD-12) to always carry a working no-cost fallback.
- (constraint) Multi-tenant isolation and Role-based money visibility are enforced server-side on every table and every egress surface, never in the interface alone (NFR-1, FR-44, FR-45, PRD section 6.2; architecture AD-2, AD-3).
- (constraint) Every profit or money figure must carry its Recognition Basis and Completeness Indicator wherever displayed or exported — visible incompleteness is deliberate and must never be smoothed away (SM-2, SM-C4; architecture AD-8).
- (constraint) One shared Insight Provider key serves every visitor to the deployment (NFR-28); the provider seam must degrade through three distinct states — absent, failing, exhausted — rather than collapsing them (architecture AD-12).
- (non-goal) No eleventh Insight Query, no model-originated figures, no conversational memory beyond the last question's period/entity, no forecasts or recommendations, no cross-Business data, no write actions from the assistant (PRD section 4.2 AI-assisted insights row).
- (non-goal) No live courier API integration, no multi-warehouse or stock transfers, no batch/lot/expiry tracking, no two-factor or SSO authentication, no per-Business provider keys in v1 (full per-module exclusion matrix in scope-caps.md companion).
- (non-goal) No scheduled or automated sending of any artifact (Daily Summary, reports), no receipt OCR, no payment plans or credit-limit enforcement, no custom report builder or saved views.
- (assumption) FR-77 Bangla browser speech recognition quality is unverified on the target device class; PRD section 12 Q10 says settle it by trying it during build, dropping to v2 under the retreat order if a hands-on check fails.
- (assumption) FR-81 model Bangla phrasing quality versus the fixed template is unmeasured; PRD section 12 Q15 says settle it by reading generated answers beside their templates, keeping the template as default if the model is not actually better.
- (assumption) NFR-28's single shared Insight Provider quota surviving a full assessment session is untested; architecture AD-12 fixes the exhausted-state behavior but not whether exhaustion will actually occur.
- (assumption) Docker build support on Render's free instance specifically is established by omission from an exclusion list, not an affirmative statement (architecture spine, deployment section); confirmed at first deploy.
- (question) Parser accuracy corpus (PRD section 12 Q1): whether a hand-built Bangla/Banglish regression fixture should be assembled, and who defends its representativeness. Owner: developer, before order capture is considered complete.
- (question) Product-purchase Expense double-counting (PRD section 12 Q2): whether Profit Summary should exclude the product-purchase Expense category by default since goods bought are already Purchase Cost at Stock-In. Owner: developer, before Profit Summary is built.
- (question) Grouped settlement rows (PRD section 12 Q4): whether match-plus-variance proves workable against synthetic fixtures, or a minimal split capability becomes unavoidable. Revisit after settlement fixtures are built.
- (question) Undetectable Expense incompleteness (PRD section 12 Q9): whether Profit Summary should carry a standing disclosure that an Expense never entered is invisible to the Completeness Indicator, or whether stating the Expense total used is sufficient. Owner: developer, before Profit Summary is built.
- (question) Demonstration/seed data (PRD section 12 Q8): how much seeded Business data the deployed instance carries and how it is labelled as seeded, so an assessor sees a populated product rather than empty screens.
