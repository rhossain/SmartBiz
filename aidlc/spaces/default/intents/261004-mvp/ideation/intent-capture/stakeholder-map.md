# Stakeholder Map

## Decision-Makers

| Stakeholder | Role | Authority | Primary Interest | Source |
|-------------|------|-----------|------------------|--------|
| Business Owner | Owner | Sole decision-maker | Profit visibility and low cost | [Q5] |

The Business Owner is the only decision-maker for this MVP run. [Q5] They decide every scope or priority change during this run; nobody else can approve a change. [Q6]

## Influencers

| Stakeholder | Role | Influence Scope | Communication | Source |
|-------------|------|-----------------|----------------|--------|
| Sales Staff (1–2 employees) | Operational input | Influences UX choices via daily order-entry feedback; not a decider | Reach out when an order-entry workflow question arises | [Q2] [Q6] |
| Inventory Staff (1–2 employees) | Operational input | Influences stock-handling UX; not a decider | Reach out when stock-handling workflow question arises | [Q2] [Q6] |

The Business Owner is also the sole influencer for non-operational decisions (architecture, prioritization, scope). [Q5] [Q6] Operational staff inform UX decisions but cannot approve changes. [Q6]

## Communication Requirements
- **Cadence**: Weekly status note in our shared channel, no formal meeting. [Q7]
- **Channel**: Shared channel (as already used by the team). [Q7]
- **Format**: Single written weekly update covering in-scope progress, blockers, and decisions taken or needed. [Q7]
- **Trigger for escalation**: Outside the weekly cadence, reach out only when something needs a decision. [Q7]
