## Contribution

I am the quality spoke for the Step 3 blind review. I read the lead's `team-practices.md` draft through the lens of testing posture, coverage tooling, CI quality gates, test/code patterns, and gaps the Step 4 interview must resolve. I disagree with the lead on several points — most importantly, the draft is silent on E2E tooling, Bangla i18n testing, NBR retention schema testing, and coverage-tool selection, and its treatment of the paste-parser test surface and the three-layer Role-gate test pyramid is underspecified for the production repo's 76-story surface area. I also think the lead has overstated the value of the 8-harness mutual-blindness design for a 1-person production team, and the 80% floor needs re-examination against the production repo's shape (not the prototype's). My overall position is **mixed**: the lead correctly carries the prototype's design-rationale culture forward, but underspecifies the production test surface in three places that will hurt the Owner in the pilot window.
