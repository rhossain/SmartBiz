### Summary

The artifacts are well-structured, the diary captures the 5 Q&A decisions as interpretations, the inline-writing deviation is honestly logged, and the design-system-mapping §3 role × capability matrix is the right binding source of truth. The trace from 39 stories to 14 mockup blocks is honestly scoped (1:1 on the 30 story screens; the 9 system / operational stories have no surface by design). The Bangla-first invariant, status-bucket math, append-only audit, and the 5 design-system gaps are all enumerated with the typed TypeScript signatures called out for Functional Design. Four blocking issues prevent READY: the runtime-facing "Viewer" 6th role that contradicts the 5-role matrix (R-01), the S7h status select that conflates directly-selectable and derived statuses (R-02), the mixed-Latin negative figure in S11 (R-03), and the missing dedicated low-stock screen for US1.9.3 (R-04). The remaining findings (R-05–R-12) are precision edits the Functional Design stage will need. With the four Critical / Major blockers addressed, the set is implementation-ready for Component Design and Functional Design.
