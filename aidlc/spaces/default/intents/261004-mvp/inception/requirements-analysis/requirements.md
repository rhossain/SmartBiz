## Intent analysis

The Owner of a small Bangladeshi SME runs daily operations across notebooks,
spreadsheets, Messenger/WhatsApp, courier portals, and manual arithmetic. The
fragmentation causes missed/duplicate orders, wrong stock, poor customer
history, COD reconciliation failures, expense/profit tracking gaps, unmanaged
dues, high return/cancellation rates, no reports, and no visibility. [intent-capture]
[intent-capture:Q1]

The product collapses those surfaces into one **Bangla-first** web app that
surfaces **two non-overlapping money figures** (earned vs realised) and
**gates money visibility by role**. The first day-one user is the Owner, with
up to two staff joining shortly after. The success metric is 10 SMEs × 50
orders in 60 days inside a 90-day pilot. [intent-capture:Q2,Q3] [scope-md §11]

The MVP scope is the 9 table-stakes modules of `docs/scope.md §5.1–§5.9` at
Standard depth, each module at full sub-feature breadth. The 3 differentiators
(Dashboard §5.10, Reports §5.11, AI Insights §5.12) are deferred to phase 2
because they need the operations layer to be live. [scope-document]

**One business per deployment.** A deployment is a single tenant with one
business profile, one Owner, and role-based staff. [interview Q7]
[scope-md §6] [team.md#Deployment]

## Functional requirements

FR IDs are stable traceability keys. Sub-requirements use `FR{n}.{m}` and
follow the parent for downstream stages. [stage-protocol §10]

### Module 5.1 — Authentication & Business Setup (FR1)

- **FR1.1** The system shall support email + password registration, sign-in,
  sign-out, and a password-recovery flow that does not depend on any paid
  third-party service. [scope-md §5.1] [scope-md §9]
- **FR1.2** The first user to complete sign-up for a deployment shall be
  granted the **Owner** role automatically. Subsequent invitations can
  assign any other role. [scope-md §5.1] [scope-md §6] [interview Q7]
- **FR1.3** The Owner shall be able to invite staff by email, choosing one
  of the four non-Owner roles (Manager, Sales Staff, Inventory Staff,
  Accountant). Invitations shall carry an expiry, a one-time redemption
  code, and a revocation path. [scope-md §5.1] [architecture §"Patterns"]
- **FR1.4** The system shall enforce a role-based access control matrix at
  three layers (router, screen-side self-gate, and an absence-asserted
  test), preserving the prototype's three-layer gate that collapsed once
  on a Role-adaptive screen with no money flag. [code-quality §6.6]
  [architecture §"Patterns"] [team.md#Testing Posture]
- **FR1.5** The system shall maintain a single business profile per
  deployment (name, address, phone, currency, default language). The
  business profile shall be the tenant boundary; no record may exist
  without a business. [scope-md §6] [interview Q7]
- **FR1.6** The system shall support user-level and business-level settings
  (display language, default Role for new sessions, business-level toggles
  for AI opt-in, and a debug-only flows surface). [scope-md §5.1]
  [architecture §"Patterns"]

### Module 5.2 — Customer Management (FR2)

- **FR2.1** The system shall record a customer profile with name, phone
  (validated as `01XXXXXXXXX` 11-digit BD format), address, free-text
  notes, and an optional basic risk score. [scope-md §5.2]
  [architecture §"Patterns"]
- **FR2.2** The customer detail screen shall display the customer's order
  history, payment history, outstanding dues, and return/cancellation
  history with the same status-bucket semantics as the orders module.
  [scope-md §5.2] [scope-md §6]
- **FR2.3** The customer list shall be searchable by name, phone, and
  partial phone, and shall expose basic risk chip on every row.
  [scope-md §5.2]
- **FR2.4** Customer notes shall be append-only; revisions shall reference
  the prior note rather than overwrite it. [scope-md §6] (audit
  discipline carries the same append-only pattern as the audit table.)

### Module 5.3 — Product Management (FR3)

- **FR3.1** The system shall record a product with name, SKU, category,
  variants (multi-axis matrix), purchase cost, selling price, current
  stock, minimum stock, and a default supplier. [scope-md §5.3]
- **FR3.2** The product detail screen shall expose a variant matrix and a
  per-variant stock display. Low-stock variants shall surface the
  suggested-supplier CTA. [scope-md §5.3] [architecture §4.
  "Restock: low-stock alert → order from supplier"]
- **FR3.3** The product creation flow shall validate SKU uniqueness per
  business, require non-negative cost and non-negative price, and reject
  any variant axis with zero values. [scope-md §5.3]

### Module 5.4 — Inventory Management (FR4)

- **FR4.1** The system shall maintain per-variant stock and shall update
  stock atomically on every order, return-restock, count correction, and
  stock-in. Stock-out is implicit (it is `current - reserved`); explicit
  stock-out only happens on count correction. [scope-md §5.4] [scope-md §6]
- **FR4.2** The system shall surface low-stock variants (any variant below
  its minimum stock) on a dedicated screen, with a link to the parent
  product and a link to the suggested supplier. [scope-md §5.4]
  [architecture §4]
- **FR4.3** Stock adjustments (count corrections, damaged stock, manual
  stock-out) shall require a reason note and shall append an audit row
  per `scope-md §6`. [scope-md §5.4] [scope-md §6]
- **FR4.4** Returned stock shall restore inventory **only when physically
  received**; a `Returned` order does not auto-restore. The
  physical-received event is a separate, audited action. [scope-md §6]

### Module 5.5 — Order Management (FR5)

- **FR5.1** The system shall support **seven** order-capture input modes
  in MVP: paste-parse, manual, repeat, quick-add, photo, spreadsheet, and
  voice. [interview Q3] [architecture §1 "Create an order via
  paste-and-parse"] [scope-md §5.5]
- **FR5.2** `parseMessage` (the paste-parse engine) shall apply the
  segment-isolation rule **verbatim** — *"an item's details come from the
  item's own words"* — and any test that asserts it shall restate the
  segment rule inline rather than import it, so a flip in the constants
  is caught. [code-quality §6.5] [team.md#Testing Posture] [architecture §1]
- **FR5.3** The order status lifecycle shall carry the full nine-value
  enum: `new`, `confirmed`, `packed`, `shipped`, `delivered`, `settled`,
  `cancelled`, `returned`, `partial`. No value is collapsed to a derived
  view and no value is left as a magic string; the production code uses
  a discriminated string-literal union with an exhaustive `switch` and a
  `never`-asserted default. [interview] [team.md#Testing Posture]
  [scope-md §5.5]
- **FR5.4** Order items shall reference a product variant and a quantity;
  per-line discount and per-order discount and delivery charge shall be
  recorded. [scope-md §5.5]
- **FR5.5** An order shall record its `source` (paste-parse, manual,
  repeat, quick-add, photo, spreadsheet, voice) and shall expose the
  source on the order detail screen for auditability. [scope-md §5.5]
- **FR5.6** A `Cancelled` order shall not count as a completed sale (it
  drops out of the realised bucket) and shall remain visible in history.
  A `Returned` order shall move to the returns bucket; if the returned
  stock is physically received, FR4.4 applies. [scope-md §6]

### Module 5.6 — COD & Delivery Management (FR6)

- **FR6.1** The system shall record a consignment per shipped order with
  courier, consignment id, COD amount, courier charge, and delivery
  status. [scope-md §5.6]
- **FR6.2** A delivered COD order shall remain **unsettled** until the
  owner records the settlement event (or, later, a settlement import).
  This is the **delivered-but-unsettled** invariant from `scope-md §6`:
  `delivered` ≠ `settled` in the money engine. [scope-md §6] [interview Q5]
- **FR6.3** A settlement run shall list every consignment for the chosen
  period with a disposition of `matched`, `short`, `unmatched`, or
  `duplicate`, and shall allow the owner to pick a variance disposition
  (`accept_partial`, `dispute`, `write_off`) per row. Every disposition
  is audited. [scope-md §5.6] [architecture §2 "Record a COD payment"]
- **FR6.4** Courier configuration (name, base charge, return-charge
  rule) shall be stored per business; the owner may add a new courier
  inline. [scope-md §5.6]

### Module 5.7 — Expense Management (FR7)

- **FR7.1** The system shall record an expense with date, amount,
  category, optional vendor, and free-text notes. [scope-md §5.7]
- **FR7.2** The expense category list shall be seedable from a fixed
  default (Product Purchase, Advertising, Courier, Packaging, Salary,
  Rent, Utilities, Other) and editable per business. [scope-md §5.7]
- **FR7.3** Expenses shall be included in the profit calculation
  (the `realized` bucket subtracts expenses) and shall surface on the
  daily card with a basis pill. [scope-md §6] [business-overview §"The
  product's thesis"]

### Module 5.8 — Customer Due Management (FR8)

- **FR8.1** Customer dues shall be a **derived view** computed from
  `Orders` + `Payments`; the system shall not store a `dues` table.
  The owner-facing "Dues" list shall show every customer with an
  outstanding balance and a per-customer due detail. [interview Q5]
  [scope-md §5.8] [scope-md §6] [architecture §3 "Collect a customer
  due"]
- **FR8.2** A "collect partial" action shall record a `Payment` ledger
  entry (amount, date, optional note) and shall reduce the derived due
  balance; the basis pill stays `earned` until the underlying order is
  courier-reconciled. [architecture §3] [interview Q5]
- **FR8.3** The dues screen shall expose due-by-customer and due-by-order
  breakdowns; the daily card shall surface total outstanding dues with a
  basis pill. [scope-md §5.8]

### Module 5.9 — Supplier Management (FR9)

- **FR9.1** The system shall record a supplier with name, phone, address,
  notes, products supplied, last purchase date, and outstanding payable.
  [scope-md §5.9]
- **FR9.2** The supplier detail screen shall expose purchase history and
  outstanding balance; "Record purchase" shall record both a supplier
  payment and the corresponding stock-in. [scope-md §5.9]
  [architecture §4]

### Cross-cutting: Recognition-basis invariant (FR10)

- **FR10.1** Every money figure in the system shall carry **(a) a basis
  pill (`earned` vs `realized`)** and **(b) a completeness control
  showing `have` of `of` with a why-missing sheet**. The basis pill is
  not optional: the design remark *"remove the basis pill and the
  numbers become opinions"* is a load-bearing constraint. [interview Q2]
  [business-overview §"The product's thesis"] [architecture §"Patterns"]
- **FR10.2** The status-bucket math (`EARNED_STATUSES`, `REALIZED_STATUSES`,
  `PIPELINE_STATUSES`) shall be restated inline in any test that asserts
  it; tests must NOT import the constants. A flip in the constants is a
  failing test, not a silent re-shape. [team.md#Testing Posture]
  [code-quality §7]
- **FR10.3** The recognition basis shall be enforced at three layers:
  **(a) the data engine** (`econ()` produces both figures from the same
  order rows), **(b) the screen surface** (`basisPill` + `completeness`
  + `basis-why`/`completeness-why` data-act hooks), and **(c) the test
  layer** (an absence-asserted harness that fails if a money screen
  renders without a basis pill). [code-quality §7] [architecture
  §"Patterns"]

### Cross-cutting: Bangla-first invariant (FR11)

- **FR11.1** Every money figure, date, and quantity shall render in
  Bangla digits (`bnDigits`) by default. The Latin-digit allow-list is
  exact: record IDs (`ORD-####`, `P-###`, `C-##`, `A-##`, `STL-####`,
  `PT-####`/`SF-#####`/`RX-#####`), phone numbers, filenames, URLs, ISO
  dates, and version strings. No other surface renders in Latin digits.
  [business-overview §"Why this product (the wedge)"] [i18n.js line
  1–4] [truth.js `LATIN_OK`]
- **FR11.2** All UI copy shall be bilingual via the `L(bn, en)` helper;
  the prototype's `L(bn, en)` signature carries forward. Switching
  language shall not reload the page. [i18n.js] [architecture §"Patterns"]
- **FR11.3** The UI shall remain functional on a 3G connection in Dhaka
  (p95 page render ≤ 3 s on a 3G network) and the Bangla font shall
  resolve offline via the system Bengali fallback chain when Google
  Fonts is unreachable. [interview Q1] [team.md#Code Style] [tokens.css]

### Cross-cutting: Audit, RBAC, security (FR12)

- **FR12.1** The audit table shall be **append-only at the database
  role level** (the application role holds `INSERT` only; `UPDATE` and
  `DELETE` are revoked at the role). CI must fail any migration that
  re-grants `UPDATE` or `DELETE` on the audit table to the application
  role. Retention is 5 years. Reversals are not deletes — they are
  NEW audit rows that reference the row being reversed. [constraint-
  register TC9] [interview] [team.md#Deployment] [scope-md §6]
- **FR12.2** The role × capability matrix is a typed discriminated
  union: `capability.money: 'full' | 'price' | 'cost'`. Manager sees
  every figure but not the audit trail; Sales Staff and Inventory Staff
  must not see profit; Accountant sees cost and price but not the
  audit-trail export. [architecture §"Patterns"] [scope-md §6]
- **FR12.3** Every money-mutating action (order create/update,
  settlement disposition, due collection, expense entry, stock
  adjustment) shall append an audit row carrying the actor, the
  business, the entity, the before/after values, and the timestamp.
  [scope-md §6] [constraint-register TC9]
- **FR12.4** Every cross-tenant or cross-role data access is forbidden
  at the row level; tests assert gating by **absence over every route**
  rather than by the presence of a lock on one route. [team.md#Testing
  Posture] [code-quality §6.6]

## Non-functional requirements

NFR IDs are stable traceability keys. [stage-protocol §10]

### NFR1 — Pilot-realistic uptime & recovery

- **NFR1.1** Staging target uptime ≥ 95 % measured monthly. No formal
  production SLA. [interview Q1]
- **NFR1.2** RPO ≤ 24 h (free-tier managed-DB daily snapshot). RTO
  ≤ 24 h (snapshot restore + free-tier hosting redeploy). [interview Q1]
- **NFR1.3** Every merge to `main` deploys to staging automatically;
  production deploys on manual approval. [team.md#Deployment]
  [constraint-register TC6]

### NFR2 — Pilot-realistic performance

- **NFR2.1** p95 page render ≤ 3 s on a 3G connection in Dhaka for any
  primary screen (splash, dashboard, orders list, order detail,
  customer detail, supplier detail, settlement review, dues list, due
  detail). [interview Q1] [scope-md §10 "performance"]
- **NFR2.2** Money-figure rendering is asserted in jsdom, not from
  screenshots. The prototype's `truth.js` analogue carries forward;
  contrast is asserted off design tokens, not by eye.
  [team.md#Testing Posture] [code-quality §1]

### NFR3 — Free-tier observability

- **NFR3.1** Application emits structured JSON logs to stdout with
  request IDs; metrics are computed from logs in a separate cron job.
  No SaaS APM, no distributed tracing. [interview Q6] [constraint-
  register TC1 TC2]
- **NFR3.2** Free-tier managed-DB dashboards cover slow-query
  monitoring. Error spikes page the Owner via a free-tier email gateway
  or a Discord/Telegram webhook the Owner owns. [interview Q6]

### NFR4 — Accessibility

- **NFR4.1** WCAG AA contrast on every money figure and every
  status-basis pill, asserted off design tokens (the prototype's
  `contrast.js` analogue). [team.md#Testing Posture] [tokens.css]
- **NFR4.2** Full keyboard reachability of every primary action on every
  primary screen; the prototype's `flows.js` analogue extends to the
  production surface.

### NFR5 — Security & compliance

- **NFR5.1** No mandatory paid third-party service. Every external
  dependency lives inside a free-tier envelope or is open-source.
  [scope-md §9] [constraint-register TC1 TC2]
- **NFR5.2** DB-level insert-only audit (NFR12.1) is the binding
  enforcement. The application code may not bypass it; CI must fail any
  migration that re-grants the revoked privileges. [constraint-register
  TC9] [interview] [team.md#Deployment]
- **NFR5.3** No live payment-gateway integration in MVP (no PCI scope).
  [constraint-register RC2] [scope-md §7]
- **NFR5.4** Manual entry for courier and payment settlement
  information until direct integrations land. [constraint-register RC3]
  [scope-md §8]

### NFR6 — Code quality & testability

- **NFR6.1** Consolidated line-coverage of custom application code
  ≥ 80 % before every merge to `main`. Coverage tooling is required in
  CI. [team.md#Testing Posture] [constraint-register TC5] [memory:M3]
- **NFR6.2** The test suite preserves the prototype's mutual-blindness
  discipline: structural/arithmetic harnesses, rendering/click
  harnesses, flow harnesses, CSS-class reachability, contrast-off-tokens
  harnesses, and story-coverage parity. Collapsing the harnesses loses
  the property. [code-quality §1] [team.md#Testing Posture]
- **NFR6.3** Test-after ordering per layer: implement data + view +
  action → write the layer's tests → run the layer's tests + the full
  suite → confirm 80 % floor still holds. [team.md#Testing Posture]
- **NFR6.4** Mutation discipline: each truth-harness assertion is
  re-checked by reintroducing the defect it was written for; each
  mutation must be caught. [team.md#Testing Posture] [code-quality §1]
- **NFR6.5** Critical regression-prone path coverage: every paste-parse
  flow test asserts the segment-isolation rule with multiple products,
  multiple variants per product, and common Bangla connector words.
  [team.md#Testing Posture] [code-quality §6.5]
- **NFR6.6** Three-layer Role-gate test: the role × capability gate is
  tested **by absence over every route**, not by the presence of a lock
  on one route. The Owner still reaches the audit trail and the daily
  card. [team.md#Testing Posture] [code-quality §6.6]
- **NFR6.7** Test-data stability: tests run against a fixed seed clock
  (the prototype's `App.TODAY = '2026-09-07'` discipline); production
  uses `new Date()` for the live system but freezes the test clock.
  [team.md#Testing Posture] [code-quality §6.10]

### NFR7 — Code style & architecture

- **NFR7.1** The production repo is full TypeScript with `"strict":
  true`, `"noImplicitAny": true`, `"noImplicitReturns": true`,
  `"noUncheckedIndexedAccess": true`, and `"exactOptionalPropertyTypes":
  true`. `// @ts-ignore` is banned without an inline justification
  comment on the same line; `// @ts-nocheck` on the whole file is
  banned. JSDoc + `// @ts-check` is **not** a fallback and is
  rejected. [interview] [team.md#Code Style]
- **NFR7.2** Modern ES uniformly: `let`/`const`, arrow functions,
  template literals, `class`, destructuring, ES modules. `var` is
  banned. The prototype's ES5/ES2015+ split is resolved to a single
  modern dialect. [team.md#Code Style] [technology-stack §"Tooling
  caveats"]
- **NFR7.3** Single top-level `package.json` (or workspace) with both
  runtime and test deps. The prototype's test-only `package.json` is
  resolved at first PR. [team.md#Code Style] [technology-stack]
- **NFR7.4** One registry per concern: `routes`, `actions`, `state` are
  typed registries (the prototype's `App.screens` / `App.actions` /
  `App.state` shape carries forward as the typed version, NOT as the
  mutable `window.App` global). [team.md#Code Style] [architecture
  §"Patterns"]
- **NFR7.5** State container must be typed: schema, ownership,
  mutation rules. The prototype's free-form mutable bag is replaced.
  [team.md#Code Style] [code-quality §6.9]
- **NFR7.6** Chrome binary path parameterised via env var or
  `chrome-launcher` so cross-platform CI can run the CDP harnesses.
  [team.md#Code Style] [technology-stack §"Tooling caveats"]
- **NFR7.7** Bundler + TypeScript transpiler (Vite or equivalent) at the
  first PR. The "opens from `file://`" property of the prototype is a
  constraint of the greenfield-to-production review, not a production
  property to preserve. [team.md#Code Style] [technology-stack
  §"Tooling caveats"]
- **NFR7.8** Comments-as-design-rationale: every module carries a
  leading block comment; inline comments document decisions, not
  syntax. No `console.log` / `TODO` / `FIXME` / dead code in shipped
  code; CI fails on any of those markers. [team.md#Code Style]
  [code-quality §4.2 §4.3 §6.11]

### NFR8 — Deployment & environment

- **NFR8.1** Trunk-based + squash-merge: every Bolt lands as one
  squash-merge commit on `main` carrying the Bolt slug in the message.
  Branches live 1–2 days max. No `feature/`/`fix/`/`hotfix/` prefixes.
  [team.md#Way of Working] [constraint-register TC4] [memory:M1]
- **NFR8.2** Staging + production, single tenant per deployment. The
  Owner runs the SME in their own deployment. [team.md#Deployment]
  [interview Q7]
- **NFR8.3** PR review is a self-review checklist (a) tests pass
  locally + on CI, (b) coverage floor met, (c) no production secrets
  committed, (d) audit-trail append-only honoured for any finance
  mutation, (e) recognition-basis invariant preserved.
  [team.md#Way of Working] [intent-capture:Q5]
- **NFR8.4** Reverse-deploy gates: a regression in coverage, in CI, or
  in the recognition-basis / role-gate / audit-immutability invariants
  blocks the merge — not just a failed unit test. The gate is enforced
  in CI, not by review. [team.md#Deployment]
