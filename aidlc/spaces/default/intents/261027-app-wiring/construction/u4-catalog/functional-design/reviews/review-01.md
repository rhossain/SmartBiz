### Summary

The design is largely implementable. Tenancy (composite business/id references, 404 for foreign ids), the role mapping (it matches the US5.1 table and `capability.ts`: Owner holds price and full, Manager full, Sales price, Accountant cost, and the any-of access type exists), the unique-violation races (SKU, note revision), the exact grants and migrations 0018–0022 (after 0017), and the no-PII audit payloads and null-cost rule all hold up against the sources. The two Majors are an ambiguous PATCH semantics for omitted customer fields (F-01) and amendments D1–D9 that the spec says are recorded but are not in the contract file (F-02). Neither makes the unit unbuildable, so the verdict is READY, with the Minors to be settled in this stage or in the Code Generation plan.
