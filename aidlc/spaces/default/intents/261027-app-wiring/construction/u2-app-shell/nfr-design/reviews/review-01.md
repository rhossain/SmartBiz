### Summary

The decisions are mostly sound: the keyset ordering, tie-break and retention boundary are right, there is no new dependency or spend, and the traceability IDs and targets resolve. NOT-READY rests on three Majors. D-01: the 401 handoff, as described, contradicts U1's shipped `session-store` and would leak `?t=`. D-02: the real-browser script cannot be implemented from the design (server and database wiring, credentials, Chrome path). D-04: the client walk's per-route assertions are undefined, so a client-gate defect could pass. The gate and inventory gaps (D-03) and the smaller points should be fixed in the same revision.
