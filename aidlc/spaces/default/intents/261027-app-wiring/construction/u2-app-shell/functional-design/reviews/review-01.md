### Summary

No Critical finding and two Major ones, so the design is implementable. The two Majors are the dependency direction (Presentation reaching into Router and client for the nav list and the 401 handoff) and the access value of / that contradicts the shipped registry and U1's gate test. Both are small spec fixes and neither blocks U3 or U4. The role-table rows match capability.ts and US5.1 for every route that exists, and every AC of US1.7, US5.1, US5.3 and US5.4 maps to at least one supporting rule. The Minor findings are mostly stale facts about what U1 already shipped and missing test obligations; fixing them at the gate keeps Code Generation from guessing.
