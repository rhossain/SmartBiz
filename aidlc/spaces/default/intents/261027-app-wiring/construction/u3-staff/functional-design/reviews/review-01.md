### Summary
The core security shape is sound: 256-bit tokens stored as hashes, identical 410 for unknown/used/expired, recover does nothing for every email, resets end all sessions and close sibling resets, accept cannot create an owner (role comes from the invitation and the table CHECK), same-token double-accept is serialised by the row lock, audit payloads carry no email, and there is no new dependency or spend. One Major gap remains (hashing and lock ordering and rate limiting on the public accept/reset routes, F-01); with a single Major and no Critical the verdict is READY, but F-01 and the concurrency items F-02 and F-04 should be fixed in the design before Code Generation.
