### Summary

The unit is implementable and mostly sound. I confirmed the claims: `tsc` is clean, lint is clean and 567 tests pass. I also traced the points you asked about. Role and business come from the session or the invitation row. Request schemas are strict. Audit rows carry no email, token or hash. Delegation covers self, Owner, Manager-on-Manager, supersede and cross-business. Recover touches no data. Tokens are hashed, and 410 is identical for every refusal. Errors are logged by SQLSTATE and constraint only. The loopback-fragment token handling and `t` read-once-then-`replaceState` are acceptable (deviation 14), since fragments are never sent and never put in Referer. The gate and mutant-runner changes only add realdb URLs to realdb-mapped mutants and strip `REALDB_CONTAINER`, so they do not weaken merge evidence. Verdict is READY with two Major findings, which is within the limit. G-01 (a sign-in racing a reset can outlive it) and G-02 (missing mutants that I showed survive the unit suite) should be fixed before the Bolt merge.
